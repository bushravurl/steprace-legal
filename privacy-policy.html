STEP RACE PRIVACY POLICY Last Updated: August 14, 2026 Step Race ("Step Race", "we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information Step Race collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you when you use the Step Race mobile application. 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT 1.1 Account Information When you create and use a Step Race account, we may process information such as: • Username • Display name • Email address • Profile/avatar information • Account identifiers • Account progress, level, coins, achievements, and similar in-app information This information is used to create and maintain your account and provide the features of Step Race. 1.2 Health and Fitness Data With your explicit authorization, Step Race may access your step count data through supported health and fitness platforms, including: • HUAWEI Health Service Kit • Android Health Connect • Other supported health platforms that may be added in the future Step Race currently requests read access to step count data when this permission is required for the selected health provider. Step Race does not use step count information for medical diagnosis, medical treatment, or other medical purposes. Health and fitness permissions are requested through the relevant platform's authorization mechanism. You can deny or revoke these permissions through the applicable device or health platform settings. 1.3 How Step Data Is Used Step count data may be processed to: • Display your daily step progress • Synchronize your step progress with your Step Race account • Calculate your progress in step-based competitions • Calculate competition rankings and leaderboards • Generate activity statistics • Record competition results • Provide achievements and other activity-related features Relevant step and competition information may be transmitted to and stored on Step Race's backend systems so these features can operate across sessions and devices. Step Race does not write or modify your Huawei Health step records through the Step read permission. 1.4 Competition and Social Information If you use Step Race's competition and social features, we may process information including: • Competitions you create or join • Competition participation • Step progress within competitions • Competition rankings and results • Friend requests and friendship relationships • Invitations • Achievements and rewards Some information, such as your display name, avatar, competition step progress, and ranking, may be visible to other users participating in the same competition or interacting with you through social features. 1.5 Technical Information We may process limited technical information necessary for the operation, security, troubleshooting, and synchronization of the App, such as: • Device/platform information • Health data source/provider • Synchronization timestamps • App and API error information • Authentication and session information 2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION We use information processed through Step Race to: • Provide and maintain user accounts • Provide step tracking and synchronization • Operate competitions and leaderboards • Provide social and friendship features • Display activity statistics and achievements • Maintain application security • Detect or prevent abuse or manipulation • Diagnose technical problems • Improve application reliability and functionality We do not use health and fitness data for advertising purposes. 3. HUAWEI HEALTH SERVICE KIT On supported Huawei devices, Step Race may use HUAWEI Health Service Kit to obtain step count information after receiving your authorization. Step Race requests only the health data permissions necessary for the relevant application functionality. For the current Huawei integration, Step Race requests read access to step count data. Huawei Health data obtained through this integration is used to provide step tracking, competitions, rankings, and related fitness functionality. Your use of Huawei services may also be subject to Huawei's own privacy policies and terms. 4. ANDROID HEALTH CONNECT On supported Android devices, Step Race may use Android Health Connect to read step count information after receiving your permission. Health Connect permissions can be reviewed or revoked through the appropriate Android settings. 5. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. Information required for account, synchronization, competition, and social functionality may be stored on systems used to operate Step Race. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. 6. DATA SHARING We do not sell your personal information or health and fitness data. Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers where necessary to operate and secure Step Race. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, integrity, or legal rights of Step Race or its users. Competition-related information may be visible to other participants as necessary to provide competition and leaderboard functionality. 7. DATA RETENTION We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Step Race, maintain legitimate operational records, comply with applicable legal obligations, and protect the security and integrity of the service. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and its purpose. 8. YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, or deletion. You may revoke access to health data through the relevant health platform or device settings. Revoking health data permission may prevent automatic step synchronization and may limit features that depend on step data. 9. ACCOUNT AND DATA DELETION Users may request deletion of their Step Race account and associated personal information. Certain information may be retained where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement purposes. 10. CHILDREN Step Race is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent or other authorization is required by applicable law. 11. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY We may update this Privacy Policy as Step Race develops or when legal, technical, or operational requirements change.
